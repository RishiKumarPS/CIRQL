Safety first

We screen everyone
so you don't have to.

CIRQL isn't open to everyone. That's the point. Our manual vetting process creates a room where you can relax and be yourself.

01

Identity verified

Every applicant submits a government ID. We verify it before approving attendance. No fake profiles, no anonymity.

02

Manual profile review

Our team reads every application. We reject anyone whose intent, tone, or answers don't align with our community values.

03

Balanced curation

We maintain intentional gender ratios and interest diversity at every event — so the room feels balanced, not lopsided.

04

On-site CIRQL hosts

Our trained hosts are present throughout the event. They facilitate, mediate, and ensure everyone has a good time.
